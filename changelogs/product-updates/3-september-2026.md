- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **GitHub Sync**: A sync paused by a change that would have removed most of your documentation can now be sorted out from the **Docs Sync** pane. Fix the repository and click **Check the repository again**, or click **Apply the change anyway** if you meant it. We also email whoever pushed the commit when a sync pauses. See [GitHub Sync](/support-center/github-sync#when-a-change-is-blocked).
