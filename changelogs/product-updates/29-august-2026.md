- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Organisation Settings**: An organisation can now share its HEAD tags and its footer, not just its CSS. Write them once under **Customisation**, then turn on **Use organisation HEAD tags** or **Use organisation footer** in each project that should pick them up. See [Organisation Settings](/support-center/organisation-settings#customisation).
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **AI Agent**: The agent can now find where a class or function is *defined* in your attached code, not only where it is mentioned. See [Self-Updating Docs](/support-center/self-updating-docs).
