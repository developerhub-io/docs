- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **Editor**: Delete a [category](/support-center/categories#deleting-categories) and everything in it from the category's own menu in the sidebar. Type "delete all" to confirm.
- {% badge text="Bug Fix" type="error" /%} **GitHub Sync**: Deleting, renaming or moving a page in %product% now updates your [draft branch](/support-center/github-sync#draft-branch) too, so a deleted page no longer comes back when you merge the draft branch.
- {% badge text="Bug Fix" type="error" /%} **Search**: Pages deleted with their category, or through the API, now leave [search](/support-center/using-search), and the [AI Assistant](/support-center/ai-search) no longer answers from them.
