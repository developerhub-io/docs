- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Team**: Organisation settings gains a Team pane where owners can see everyone in the organisation, sort by name or last active, and deactivate a member instead of removing them. A deactivated member can no longer sign in, but stays credited on everything they wrote in [page history](/support-center/page-history) and the [activity log](/support-center/activity-log).
