- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Activity Log**: Your organisation's [activity log](/support-center/organisation-settings#activity-log) shows what each change came through, such as "via Claude Code" or "via MCP key “CX automation”", and finds changes by it. The [audit log API](/v1.0/api/ref#get-audit-log) carries it too.
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **Editor**: A [documentation section](/support-center/managing-documentation#publishing-documentation) or [version](/support-center/managing-versions#publishing-versions) with nothing published in it now stays published, and you can delete the last page, section or version. When you unpublish or delete the last published page of a section, the editor asks whether to unpublish the section too.
- {% badge text="Bug Fix" type="error" /%} **GitHub Sync**: Every change to a page's draft now reaches your [draft branch](/support-center/github-sync#draft-branch), including edits from the [Editor MCP Server](/support-center/editor-mcp-server) and the [AI Agent](/support-center/ai-agent).
