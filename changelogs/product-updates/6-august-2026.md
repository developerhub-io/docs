- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Index Icons**: Give any entry in your navigation an icon, so the pages readers look for most stand out. [Index icons](/support-center/index-icons) work on pages, categories, labels and links, and you can pick from Font Awesome or upload your own square image. Icons sync through your repository in `_nav.yaml`, and the index keeps its flush layout until you use one.
- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Export a single page to PDF**: PDF now sits alongside Markdoc, Markdown and Word in a page's [export menu](/support-center/exporting-documentation#exporting-a-page-to-pdf), and downloads straight away. You get the page on its own, without the navigation, table of contents, or the covers of a full [version PDF](/support-center/pdf-export), with tabs and accordions laid out open so nothing stays hidden.
- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Move a category to another documentation**: Regrouping content across sections no longer means moving pages one at a time. Moving a [category](/support-center/categories) takes everything under it along, keeping the order, and links to the pages inside are repointed for you.
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **Printing**: Printing a page from the browser now gives you the page and nothing else, on your published docs as well as in the editor. The navigation, table of contents, and the copy button on code blocks stay out of the printed copy, long code and wide tables wrap instead of being cut off at the paper edge, and pages print light even when you are reading in dark mode.
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **Font Awesome 7**: The icon set behind [inline icons](/support-center/icons) and index icons moved from Font Awesome 5 to Font Awesome 7 Free, which more than doubles what you can choose from. Existing icons keep rendering under their old names.
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **Agent Skills**: The [`organize-docs-repo` skill](/support-center/write-markdoc-with-ai) now carries the full list of valid icon names and the exact YAML quoting and indentation the sync writes back, so an agent editing your repository stops producing navigation that gets reformatted on the next sync.
