- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **UI**: Meet [Matcha](/support-center/customising-visuals#matcha-ui), a third layout for your docs with a one-row top bar and a tree index. New projects start on it, and you can try it on your own docs by adding `?ui=3` to any page.

- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Customisation**: On Matcha, the top bar can take your page colour instead of your header colour, with its own [logo for dark backgrounds](/support-center/customising-visuals#changing-logo).
- {% badge text="Improvement" /%} **Customisation**: The top bar preview in [Customisation](/support-center/customising-visuals) now shows your navigation links as you type them.
- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Theme**: Set your docs' [theme](/support-center/theme) to **Auto** and each reader sees light or dark to match their system setting.
