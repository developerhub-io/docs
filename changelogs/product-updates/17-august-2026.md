- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Feedback**: Every feedback setting now sits together under Project Settings → General → Feedback: the reactions widget, written messages, redacting personal information and flagging spam. See [Feedback](/support-center/feedback#feedback-settings).
- {% badge text="Improvement" type="info" /%} **AI Features**: Our three AI products now have three distinct names, so it is clear which one you are reaching for. [AI Agent](/support-center/ai-agent) writes and edits across your pages, [AI Writing Tools](/support-center/ai-writer) rewrite a selection in place, and [AI Assistant](/support-center/ai-search) answers your published readers.
- {% badge text="Bug Fix" type="error" /%} **AI Agent**: Saving a batch of changes that deletes a page now saves the rest of the batch as well. The deleted page also drops out of your readers' search results, and stays deleted in a [Git-synced](/support-center/github-sync) project. See [AI Agent](/support-center/ai-agent#reviewing-what-it-staged).
