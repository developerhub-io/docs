- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Project Settings**: You can now switch off the recurring broken links report email for a whole project, rather than asking every subscriber to unsubscribe. It stays on by default, and admins turn it off under Project Settings → **Advanced** → **Notifications**. See [Advanced Settings](/support-center/advanced-settings#broken-links-report-email).
