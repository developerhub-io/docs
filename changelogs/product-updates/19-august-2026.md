- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Project Settings**: You can now switch off the recurring broken links report email for a whole project, rather than asking every subscriber to unsubscribe. It stays on by default, and admins turn it off under Project Settings → **Advanced** → **Notifications**. See [Advanced Settings](/support-center/advanced-settings#broken-links-report-email).

- {% badge text="New" type="success" /%} **Project Settings**: You can now take the version picker out of your published docs' top navigation, so a project that only ever has one version doesn't show a dropdown for it. Switch it on under Project Settings → **Customisation** → **Look and feel**, and the preview beside it shows the result before you save. Your versions, their publish state and their `/v1.0/...` links are unaffected. See [Managing Versions](/support-center/managing-versions#hiding-the-version-picker).
