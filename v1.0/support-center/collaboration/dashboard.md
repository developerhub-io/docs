Dashboard is where every teammate should start their day.

{% image url="../../../assets/editor-dashboard.png" /%}

## What is in the Dashboard?

The dashboard helps your teammates find upcoming items as fast as possible. Its **Overview** tab shows:

- A count of the versions, documentation sections, API references and pages in the project.
- **Comments**: the unresolved comments that are assigned to you, and other recent threads.
- **Draft pages**: pages that have not been published yet.
- **AI agent runs**: what the [AI Agent](../self-updating-docs.md) proposed on your pull requests, when Self-Updating Docs is set up.
- **Activity**: the past 7 days of the [activity log](../activity-log.md), so you keep up with your teammates.

Two more tabs sit next to Overview:

- **Analytics**: [search analytics](../search-analytics.md). Understand how readers are using your search bar, and whether they are finding what they're looking for.
- **Feedback**: read and analyse [feedback](../feedback.md) trends on your pages.

## How to get to the Dashboard?

To open the dashboard:

- In the editor top navigation, open the scope picker and choose **Dashboard**.

You can also open Project Settings → **Insights** and click **Open dashboard**.
